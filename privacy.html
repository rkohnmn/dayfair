PLAIN LANGUAGE / PRIVACY

It's your household.
It's your data.

Dayfair does its calculations in your browser. It has no account system, server-side database, analytics scripts, trackers, advertisements, or external libraries.

What stays in your browser

Names, dates, absences, bill amounts and payer assignments are stored in your browser's localStorage on this device so they survive a refresh. The calculator reads them locally. We do not send these inputs to an API or an operator-run server. The site itself is served by your GitHub Pages host, which may keep standard web-server logs governed by its own policies.

What leaves your device

Nothing from your household records is sent automatically. If you use “Save backup” or “Download report,” your browser creates a file on your device. “Copy summary” copies text to the clipboard when you request it; “Print / PDF” invokes your browser's print dialog. You decide whether to send those files or text to anyone else.

Deletion and limits

Use “Delete saved data” in the calculator to replace the saved household with a fresh starter. You can also clear the website's storage through your browser settings. Browser storage is not encrypted by Dayfair, may be accessible to other people using your browser profile, and may be erased by private browsing, storage cleanup, or a browser reset. Back up anything important. The saved theme preference is separate from the household data.

What the calculator means

Usage is modeled with an equal share of each day's charge among residents marked present; it is not based on individual meter readings. Fixed service charges use the rule you choose on each bill. Entered dates are inclusive. This is a household agreement aid, not a legal determination of lease liability, bill accuracy, or actual energy usage.
