# Launch plan — small, honest and testable

## Day 0: readiness

1. Change the repository name / owner fields after publication as appropriate; ensure Pages builds successfully from Actions.
2. Repeat real-browser checks on the live Pages URL, especially storage, download, clipboard permission, import, printing, keyboard and mobile (see `docs/QA.md`).
3. Check search-brand conflicts and add absolute canonical / Open Graph URLs **only after** the public URL is known.
4. Test the worksheet with real billing statements that have different cycles, after obtaining consent and keeping them out of the public repo.

## First distribution

Lead with an actual problem example rather than a general tool directory: “A $180 electricity bill includes a $30 service fee; two residents were home all month but one was away a week, and another arrived on the 16th. See the calculation.” The app already loads a similar case.

Appropriate, non-spam channels: relevant roommate and student-renter groups **when tools/links are allowed**, open-source / indie hacker communities, and a single technically detailed launch write-up describing the exact per-day algorithm and browser privacy tradeoffs. Avoid unsolicited posts into disputes. Invite readers to report **how their agreement differs**; a calculator should not impose one definition of fairness.

## Find the product's actual place

Ask five households to bring anonymized bill periods and solve them live; track without surveillance: Did users understand fixed versus usage? Did they find an uncovered day? Can they explain their outputs to housemates? Are the suggested transfers accepted? Did someone request saved bill templates? Record anonymous opt-in feedback manually, not claimed conversion metrics.

If repeat users request a payment log, add a local-only “mark transfer completed” ledger with export. If people require real collaboration, evaluate a *separate* paid/sustainable hosting product without weakening the existing static free core. Commercialization is hypothetical; no demand or revenue has been demonstrated.
